## Program pricing — three-installment model

- The timing is **at enrollment → when classes start → one calendar month after classes start**. The latest user correction supersedes equal thirds: use rounded-thousand payments, e.g. **BDT 8,000 + 8,000 + 9,000** for BDT 25,000. Installment cards appear only in the program **Pricing** tab: total-fee panel on the left, three milestone cards on the right, stacked on mobile. Catalog cards and the Overview tab do not contain installment charts; enrollment offers give a text explanation and a link to the Pricing breakdown.
- The first two amounts are each one third of the total rounded down to **BDT 1,000**; the final installment covers the remaining balance without changing the total. Current BDT 12,000 and 15,000 fees split as **4,000 / 4,000 / 4,000** and **5,000 / 5,000 / 5,000**. The strict shared pricing model validates integer totals and never fabricates zero amounts for malformed fees. Home/FAQ copy explains the same timing; the independent recorded-course membership is unchanged.
- This is a proposed display model, not a payment ledger, collection system or approval of live financial terms. Existing synthetic transactions are unchanged. Confirmed cohort dates, provider integration and financial approval are still required before collection; enrollment and payment remain disabled.

## First launch — monthly bootcamp and demo checkout

- User-confirmed planning rules: launch from **January 2027**; each bootcamp lasts **12 weeks**, starting on the **first Friday on or after the 15th**, at **08:30 PM Bangladesh time**. Show **two consecutive enrollment-eligible monthly intakes**. Enrollment closes **five calendar days before the first class at 11:59 PM Asia/Dhaka** (not five 24-hour periods before the 20:30 class).
- Initial intakes: **January starts 15 January 2027; closes 10 January, 11:59 PM; total BDT 21,000, paid as 7,000 / 7,000 / 7,000**. **February starts 19 February 2027; closes 14 February, 11:59 PM; total BDT 25,000, paid as 8,000 / 8,000 / 9,000**. The next eligible intake gets **BDT 4,000 off** the BDT 25,000 standard fee; the following intake is standard price. These are total program fees, not extra admission charges.
- Installment timing is enrollment → actual first class → **one calendar month after that first class**, independent of the following cohort’s start date. January’s third installment is planned for **15 February**, not 19 February. The Pricing selector preserves the chosen intake in `?intake=YYYY-MM`, with amounts, discount, enrollment cutoff and due dates shown consistently.
- `server/launch-plan.js` is a pure, date-aware public planning model. After an intake’s enrollment cutoff passes, it drops out and the next two eligible months are shown; the next eligible intake gets the discount. The static Vercel demo freezes this at build time and needs a rebuild to advance. No existing order/receipt is repriced. Before real checkout, prices must be captured in immutable server-side quotes/orders and rules for already-paid learners and any price changes approved.
- Launch cohort identifiers use `research-bootcamp-YYYY-MM` and are **not persisted enrollment cohorts**. Each new launch plan starts with **0 synthetic enrollments / 30 planned places**, not the old November learner. Existing November cohorts, student work, teaching assignments and paid demo transactions are preserved and still power the legacy private portal. Launch plans deliberately cannot reserve seats or grant entitlements; real enrollment needs persistent per-intake capacity and transaction controls.
- The user selected **Demo checkout for now** rather than a real gateway. **Enrol** links on cohort/pricing panels open `/enrol/research-bootcamp/:YYYY-MM`, a read-only review showing the selected total, discount, first installment and remaining balance. No personal/card/wallet inputs, gateway redirect, simulated paid success, order, seat hold or enrollment is created. The payment action is disabled. Amounts and intake membership are validated from public program data; URL `amount`/`paid` flags are ignored, and expired/invented keys show an explanation.
- The new demo checkout is a narrowly allowlisted public summary in hosted previews, using the existing public snapshot. Legacy checkout/account/private routes remain blocked. Local `POST /api/enrollment/launch-checkout` returns **501** without financial/seat writes; hosted writes remain **405**. Merchant setup, privacy/identity, approved policies, verified provider callbacks, server-side totals, atomic capacity/settlement and explicit launch approval are still required for real payment collection.

## Cohort planning and detailed program curriculum

- The **Cohorts** tab leads with **Class size**, **Places left**, and **Last enrollment date**. Launch bootcamps now show **30 maximum / 30 planned places / 0 enrolled** with the confirmed deadline rule above. Internal capacity/active/pending/hold arithmetic and snapshot dates are under collapsed **How places are counted** details. For stored non-launch cohorts, the shared server helper counts active/pending seats, excludes expired/invalid holds, deduplicates held people, excludes already-allocated learners, clamps remaining seats at zero and flags over-capacity records. Only aggregate counts are public; hosted counts are synthetic build-time snapshots.
- Stored cohorts have a nullable `enrollment_deadline`, added via an idempotent, non-destructive migration. Unset legacy/non-launch deadlines still say **To be announced**; the new bootcamp launch deadlines follow the explicit five-day rule. Dates render in **Asia/Dhaka**; past deadlines do not enable enrollment. Timestamp parsing is timezone-qualified. No new admin deadline-editing or binding financial-approval workflow is introduced.
- Weekend classes start at **08:30 PM Asia/Dhaka (UTC+06:00)**, with a proposed two-hour block ending **10:30 PM**. Duration, holiday exceptions, faculty and platform arrangements require confirmation. The planner fits contact hours inside each cohort window and flags hours that cannot fit. Each launch bootcamp has **24 sessions / 48 contact hours**. Statistics retains **10 sessions / 20 hours**. The old November bootcamp’s **6 November–23 January** sample calendar remains historical/private, not the public launch intake.
- Independent workload uses existing program values: **6–8 hours/week** for the bootcamp and **4–5** for Statistics, separate from live contact time. Sunday–Thursday practice and **Thursday 11:59 PM Bangladesh time** submissions are clearly labelled proposals, not active due dates. Actual assignment-outline counts are queried from the published curriculum; final deadlines, pass marks, attendance rules, feedback turnaround and extension/resubmission policies remain unapproved.
- **Courses** now contains detailed proposed plans for all four existing bootcamp courses and both Statistics courses: learning outcomes, topic breakdowns, exercises, preparation, final deliverables and feedback criteria. Bootcamp sequencing is weeks **1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6 / 7–12**, with **8 / 8 / 8 / 24** contact hours, summing to 48. The same learning plan appears on each course detail page. Expand/collapse-all and keyboard-accessible course controls work. `#cohorts`, `#pricing` and `#curriculum` deep links open the appropriate tab.
- The plans are **draft curriculum**, not completed recordings, guaranteed mastery or approved staffing. Existing lesson records are labelled sample modules/lessons with sample runtime, not the full syllabus. Academic Writing still has no published courses or open cohorts; the UI does not invent them. No new playback, payment, enrollment, real data collection or certificate entitlement is enabled.
- Sources: `server/program-planning.js` (draft syllabi and weekend planning), `server/catalog.js` (aggregate seats), public/enrollment routes, and strict runtime-validated UI in `client/src/components/programs/`. Fresh synthetic seeds use the new Friday/Saturday evening times for the two sample calendar events. Existing stored sessions, submissions, announcements and historical financial records are **not overwritten** by this change; the full public timetable is a planning proposal, not a migration of the private calendar.

## Scholarship & Application Planner — local MVP

- Learners can open `/app/scholarships` from the dashboard or sidebar. Strict TypeScript UI modules live under `client/src/components/scholarships/`, including runtime API parsing, profile form, reference cards and tracker sheets. Profile/discovery/tracker sections are URL-addressable with `?view=profile`, `?view=discover` and `?view=tracker`.
- `server/routes/scholarships.js` stores profiles and tracker entries in separate SQLite tables, always scoped to the authenticated account (including staff using their own learner workspace). Mentor-only and finance-only accounts cannot access this module. Responses are non-cacheable; writes validate fields and consent and reject cross-site browser requests. Existing production identity/CSRF/security gates still apply.
- Academic context includes citizenship, qualification, intended degree/subject, destinations, original-scale grades, supported English-test scores, work months and research experience. **Only target degree and destination determine preference matches.** Other context generates checks, not eligibility decisions or admission probabilities. Blank optional numeric fields stay null; grades are not normalised across scales.
- Six real provider reference pages were reviewed on October 31, 2026: Chevening, Erasmus Mundus, DAAD, Study in Sweden, University Admissions Sweden and uni-assist. The curated registry is `server/scholarships/catalog.js`; rules and validation are in `server/scholarships/matcher.js`. These are **overview/directory/portal references, not six verified scholarship calls**. No current deadline, open status, funding amount or citizenship eligibility is asserted. Expanding the registry also requires updating the frontend official-host allowlist.
- Students can save one tracker entry per reference, record a self-reported status, personal reminder date and notes, then remove an entry or confirm deletion of all active planner data. Portal-specific programmes/intakes can be recorded in notes; separate per-programme/per-intake applications are future work. No application submission, file upload, provider profile-sharing or notification delivery occurs.
- Use **synthetic information only**. Consent is required on each profile save. Deletion removes active profile/tracker rows, not the academy account, learning records, external backups or minimal action-only audit events. Audit events deliberately exclude academic values and planning notes. Account-scoped storage is not a production-grade private document vault.
- Before real student use: approve data retention/privacy/security and access policies; expand the country/test coverage; introduce maintained individual calls with source evidence, intakes, deadlines and versioned eligibility rules; implement curation/review tooling and stale-source handling; evaluate matching quality; and connect approved notification integrations if wanted.

## Learner-success tools — working local pilot

- **Learner routes:** `/app/growth` (guided path), `/app/portfolio` (private evidence), `/app/career` (support and existing internship applications), `/app/alumni` (opt-in moderated pilot). They are linked in the learner sidebar, dashboard, public roadmap and `/demo-guide`. Cohort learning and the recorded-course proposal remain separate.
- **Guided learning:** one four-step introductory research-protocol path has short text lessons, draft Bangla explanations, practice prompts, an editable goal and weekly pace. Saved notes and self-reported completion are account-scoped. The server enforces sequential prerequisites and prevents reopening an earlier step while later tasks remain complete. Completion is explicitly **not assessed mastery** and grants no certificate, subscription or alumni eligibility. Expert review, target-learner testing, richer worked examples, equivalent assessments and approved content are still required before a real learning offer.
- **Private portfolio and review:** learners create baseline/capstone/application text artifacts with contribution and limitation statements. Private drafts are invisible to reviewers. Learners explicitly share each review request with a named reviewer who must have a current mentor/supervisor role and an active teaching assignment overlapping the learner's active cohort. Staff roles alone cannot read or grade arbitrary portfolios. Submitted work is frozen until withdrawal or feedback; reviewers record 0–2 criterion rubrics, actionable text and a reviewed/changes-requested decision. Reviewed snapshots and feedback remain attached to the earlier revision when a learner revises. Stale-revision reviews, duplicate feedback, cross-account edits and unassigned/self-review are rejected. Learners can delete an artifact and its feedback/snapshots.
- **Support and application clinics:** consented, bounded enquiries for CV/portfolio, research statement, interview practice, accessibility, affordability or peer-group support appear in `/admin/learner-success` for academic-ops/admin/owner accounts. Operations can respond and close open requests; learners can read replies or cancel their own pending enquiries. Maximum five pending enquiries per account. This is local triage, **not** a clinic booking, email notification, funded aid decision or bundled support entitlement. No sensitive documents, attachments, ghostwriting or professional advice is requested.
- **Internships:** the existing publication/application workflow is reused. Publication now additionally requires paid/unpaid disclosure, pay details, workload, location, named supervisor and a complaints contact; the list, detail and application queries hide incomplete legacy records. These terms are visible on public cards/detail pages and editable in content curation. Hosts still require the existing approved partner/agreement gate. No synthetic roles or partnerships are inserted, no employer transmission occurs and no placement is guaranteed. Real verification, legal/safeguarding review, no-fee enforcement, partner contracts and role monitoring remain operational prerequisites.
- **Alumni:** no members are seeded. An issued synthetic certificate gates requests; self-reported practice or feedback cannot mint eligibility. An explicit alias/interest-only opt-in stays pending until a different academic-ops/admin/owner moderator approves it. The directory is member-only and requires current eligibility plus approval; revoked certificates immediately hide access/listings. Editing requeues moderation, withdrawal deletes the listing/consent, and operations can remove unsafe listings. Obvious email/web/phone patterns are rejected and no contact or message tools are enabled. This demonstrates the workflow, **not** a verified real alumni network. Production completion rules, community policies, safety reporting/blocking, trained moderation and contact controls must precede real participation.
- **Evidence and privacy:** `/admin/learner-success` reports labelled synthetic operational counts: saved goals, all-step self-completions, saved artifacts and reviewed/unreviewed artifacts. It does not expose portfolio text, invent gains, hide missing reviews or calculate employment outcomes. Private feedback history supports human comparison, but equivalent baseline/post tasks, turnaround evaluation, cost-to-serve, affordability, satisfaction, renewals and real outcomes are not yet measured. All new API responses are non-cacheable; writes reject cross-site browser requests, validate allowlisted fields, enforce ownership/roles and log actions without work text. Confirmed full learner-success deletion removes goals, notes, artifacts/reviews, support requests and alumni consent, without deleting cohort, scholarship or existing internship records. Failed saves preserve drafts; a successful deletion followed by a failed refresh hides stale data.
- **Architecture:** strict UI/runtime parsers in `client/src/components/growth/`; `/api/growth` routes in `server/routes/growth.js`; pathway/review/validation helpers in `server/growth.js`; six separate `growth_*` tables in `server/db.js`. No new access, payment, public portfolio, real graduation, streaming, referral, job, funding or service entitlement is created. Real student use remains blocked by the existing privacy, identity, retention, security, staffing, commercial and deployment approvals.

### Independent recorded courses — new product proposal

- **Courses** is a primary desktop/mobile header link to `/courses`. It lists four clearly labelled synthetic independent deep-topic syllabi, with topic/level/language/search URL filters, custom topic-specific SVG artwork, counts, retry/empty states and `/courses/recorded/:slug` detail pages. Existing programme-linked `/courses/:slug` pages remain unchanged and do not become subscription courses.
- The proposed membership is **BDT 2,000** for every approved independent recorded course while active. **Monthly billing is an assumption, not a user-confirmed billing period or approved financial offer.** `/app/courses` is the learner library, and `/app/subscription` explains the proposal and current inactive state. Cohort programmes, one-time access grants, live mentoring and certificates remain separate. No membership, charge, saved card, renewal or cancellation is created.
- `/mentor/courses` is the instructor studio. Authorised instructors can create and edit their own private course metadata, learning outcomes and estimated lesson outlines. Draft ownership is server-enforced; descriptive fields are validated, transactional and audited. Demo catalog entries are read-only, and drafts are excluded from the public catalog. Actual recording uploads, rights/quality review, publication and protected streaming are **not connected**.
- Strict UI modules are under `client/src/components/courses/`. Backend routes are under `/api/recorded-courses` in `server/routes/recorded-courses.js`, backed by a separate `recorded_courses` SQLite table. `server/recorded-courses.js` centralises the proposed plan, validation and idempotent synthetic catalogue setup for new and existing local databases. The existing cohort curriculum tables and enrollment entitlements are not repurposed.
- Instructor economics are an **unapproved watch-time proposal**, not an earnings promise: net subscription revenue × approved creator-pool percentage × instructor eligible watch minutes / all eligible watch minutes in a settlement period. The studio calculator uses user-editable hypothetical inputs (including an explicitly unapproved 50% example pool), creates no financial record and labels its result **not payable**. Real watch minutes and recorded-course earnings return null, not fabricated zeros. Existing live-teaching demo earnings are clearly separated.
- Subscription checkout, playback-token issuance, watch-event ingestion and payout endpoints fail closed with 501 after authentication; learner requests cannot access creator mutations. No click, play request or client-supplied paid flag grants access or credits earnings. Public responses contain syllabi only, with no media credentials.
- Before live use: confirm subscription billing period and included scope; approve renewal/cancellation/refund/tax terms; choose protected streaming and payment providers; implement verified entitlements and fraud-resistant eligible-viewing measurement (including self-view/loop caps); approve creator-pool percentage, net-revenue deductions, reconciliation/dispute rules, content rights, instructor contracts, KYC/tax and payout policies. No guaranteed passive income is asserted.
